## 二、 設施與生存機能交叉比對 (Facilities Matrix)

### 1. 寢具與保暖
- **有提供厚棉被與枕頭（免帶睡袋）**：[`dongpu`](cabins/dongpu.md)
- **需自備或向協作租借高山睡袋**：[`paiyun`](cabins/paiyun.md)、[`tianchi`](cabins/tianchi.md)、[`sanliujiu`](cabins/sanliujiu.md)、[`kuaigu`](cabins/kuaigu.md)

### 2. 電力與充電
- **有提供 110V 插座充電**：[`dongpu`](cabins/dongpu.md)
- **無插座（須自備大容量行動電源）**：[`paiyun`](cabins/paiyun.md)、[`tianchi`](cabins/tianchi.md)、[`sanliujiu`](cabins/sanliujiu.md)、[`kuaigu`](cabins/kuaigu.md)

### 3. 特殊急救配置 (高山病加壓艙 PAC)
- **配置有 PAC**：[`paiyun`](cabins/paiyun.md)、[`tianchi`](cabins/tianchi.md)、[`sanliujiu`](cabins/sanliujiu.md)、[`kuaigu`](cabins/kuaigu.md)
- **緊鄰公路快速車行撤退**：[`dongpu`](cabins/dongpu.md)

### 4. 沐浴與乾燥設施
- **有熱水淋浴（投幣式）**：[`dongpu`](cabins/dongpu.md)
- **有專屬烘鞋乾燥室**：[`tianchi`](cabins/tianchi.md)
- **僅提供走廊防雨掛勾**：[`paiyun`](cabins/paiyun.md)、[`kuaigu`](cabins/kuaigu.md)、[`sanliujiu`](cabins/sanliujiu.md)
